Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we collect when you call or visit this website, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you call our line, we may collect your phone number, your address, the details you give about the problem and the recording of the call. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

This website has no forms, so we do not collect information through the site itself. Standard server logs and analytics may record your visit, such as the pages you view and your browser type.

How we use it

We use call information to dispatch a plumber, to confirm the details of the job, to improve service and to train staff. We use website logs to keep the site running and to understand which pages are useful.

Sharing

We share call details with the licensed plumber who is sent to your address so the job can be done. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Your choices

If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call. You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@247plumberkeizer.com. We respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom of the footer shows the year of the latest update. Questions can go to contact@247plumberkeizer.com.
