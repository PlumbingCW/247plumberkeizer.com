When a clogged drain is an emergency

If sewage is coming up in a tub, shower or floor drain, treat it as an emergency. Our 24/7 line sends a plumber the same day when a crew is free, and nights and weekends are covered. While you wait, stop running water, keep the toilets unflushed and keep kids and pets out of the wet area.

A single slow sink is a daytime job. A backup that spreads from one fixture to the next means the blockage sits in the main line, and that is when waiting costs you a flooded floor.

What drain cleaning means at your door

Drain cleaning is the removal of whatever is narrowing a pipe: grease, hair, soap scum, food waste, paper, wipes or tree roots. In Keizer, OR, most calls fall into three groups. A single fixture clogs, several fixtures slow down together, or the main line to the street stops.

Which group you are in decides the tool. A hand auger or small cable machine clears a trap or a branch line. A larger cable machine reaches the main line. When the pipe is coated rather than blocked, hydro jetting scours the walls with high pressure water.

Signs the clog is deeper than the sink

These patterns point past the fixture and into the building drain. Mention any of them when you call.

Gurgling after a flush

Air pushed back through the traps means the main line is partly blocked downstream.

Two fixtures clog at once

A tub and a toilet that slow down together share a branch, so the blockage is beyond both.

Floor drain bubbles

The lowest drain in the home is the first place a stopped main line shows itself.

Repeat clogs every few weeks

A pipe that keeps clogging often has a rough joint, a belly or a root in it, and a camera look finds out which.

Kitchen sink clogs: the most common call

Kitchen drains clog on grease and food solids. Grease runs liquid and then cools on the pipe wall, where it catches coffee grounds and rice and hardens into a plug. Scrape plates into the trash, wipe pans before washing and never pour cooking fat down the drain.

If a double sink drains one side and holds the other, the blockage usually sits at the tee past the shared trap. A garbage disposal that hums but leaves water standing is a different problem, covered under garbage disposal repair.

Snaking, jetting and when each one fits

Drain snaking uses a rotating cable with a cutting head. It pushes through a blockage and pulls the debris back out. It is the right first step for hair, paper and soft clogs, and it is quick.

A snake can leave a thin coat of grease on the pipe wall. When the same drain clogs again within a few weeks, hydro jetting is the better fix because it washes the full diameter clean. The trade-off is cost and setup time, so we match the tool to what the pipe needs.

A line that leaks rather than clogs is a different job. A wet spot along a drain run, a smell of sewer in a crawlspace or a patch of soft lawn points to a broken pipe. That is a repair, and a camera inspection confirms it before anyone digs.

Code and manufacturer rules that apply to drain work

Drain work in Oregon falls under the Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code, which is the state’s adoption of the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). The code expects accessible cleanouts so a main line can be cleared without cutting into walls or floors. If your home has no cleanout near the main drain, adding one is a common recommendation.

Drain machines and cleaning products also come with manufacturer instructions, and those set the limits for cable size, speed and chemical use. If a line needs more than cleaning, such as a new cleanout or a pipe section, ask whether the work needs a permit from your local building office.
