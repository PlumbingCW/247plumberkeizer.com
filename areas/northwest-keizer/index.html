How the City of Keizer outlines Northwest Keizer

The city draws this neighborhood with Chemawa Road as the southern edge and River Road as the eastern edge. Lockhaven marks the north, and Windsor Island Road runs along the west out to the city limits.

Keizer Elementary is part of the city description. So are Log House Gardens and the Wildwood Villa manufactured home park. We use those names only to help you place yourself on the map, not to say anything about the pipes in them.

Manufactured homes and general plumbing layout

Because the city description mentions a manufactured home park, one general note is worth making. Homes built on a different frame from a typical house can have pipes laid out in different places, such as under the floor or in a utility closet. That is a general point about home types and not a finding about this park.

If you live in a manufactured home, tell us when you call. It helps the plumber plan what tools to bring and where to look first.
