Where West Keizer sits

The City of Keizer describes West Keizer by its edges. Chemawa Road forms the north side, River Road the east side, and the Willamette River the west. The south edge is the line where Keizer meets Salem.

The city counts about 3,100 households in this area. Its neighborhood description also lists Cummings Elementary and five parks. That is a large and varied area, so a street-level detail from one block should not be assumed for the next.

When you call, a cross street such as Chemawa Road or River Road helps us place the address. If your home sits right at the southern edge, the Salem page explains how we handle addresses on the other side of that line.

A river on one side and what that does and does not tell us

The Willamette River is the west edge of this neighborhood. Keizer as a whole sits on the river flood plain, and the city marks floods in 1861, 1890, 1943, 1964 and 1996 at Keizer Rapids Park. That is a citywide fact and not a finding about any West Keizer street.

As general plumbing information, a house in a wet low spot can have a damp crawl space, and a damp crawl space is a place where small supply leaks go unnoticed. If you see a spike in your water bill or a musty smell under the floor, a leak check is a sensible first call.

Before you call about a plumbing problem

This is general guidance for any home and is not specific to West Keizer.

  • ✓
    Find your main shutoff. Know where the whole-house valve is before a pipe fails.
  • ✓
    Take a photo. Show the leak, the fixture or the water heater label so the description is exact.
  • ✓
    Note what changed. A new noise, smell, stain or slow drain tells us where to start.
  • ✓
    Skip chemical drain openers. They can harm pipes and make later work less safe.
