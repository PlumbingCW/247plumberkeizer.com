Before we arrive in Woodburn: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak

Four steps protect your home in the minutes before the plumber arrives. They take less than five minutes and they limit the damage whatever the cause.

  • ✓
    Close the main shutoff. Find the valve where the water line enters the house, often in a garage or crawlspace, and turn it clockwise.
  • ✓
    Open the lowest tap. It drains the lines and relieves pressure.
  • ✓
    Kill power to wet rooms. Only if the breaker is dry and easy to reach. If not, stay out and tell dispatch.
  • ✓
    Call the 24/7 line. Give the address, what failed and whether anyone is in danger.

Water running

Close the main shutoff first, then call. Moving water does the most damage in the first ten minutes.

Sewage backing up

Stop all water use, avoid flushing and keep people out of the room. See drain cleaning.

No hot water

Turn off power or gas to the heater, close its cold valve and call. See water heater repair.

Unknown source

Check the meter with every tap off. A moving dial means a leak, and leak detection finds it.

What the Woodburn water supply means for your plumbing

The City of Woodburn draws its water from the Troutdale Aquifer through six active wells, and treats it at three plants that remove iron, manganese, arsenic and radon. It adds chloramines afterward. Its 2024 water quality report says Woodburn’s fluoride is natural and that the city does not add it.

Because iron and manganese are removed at the plant, orange or black staining at your fixtures more often points to old galvanized pipe or a tired water heater in your own house. That is worth a camera look or a tank inspection rather than a filter purchase.

The same report lists sodium at 24.3 mg/L in 2017, above the 20 mg/L level it flags for people on a restricted sodium diet. If anyone in your home is on one, ask your doctor before adding a salt-based water softener.

Keizer notes

An old railroad town with newer neighborhoods

Jesse Settlemier founded Woodburn in 1863, and the town was platted in 1871 after the railroad arrived. It was incorporated in 1889, so the oldest lots near the historic core carry plumbing that is well over a century in the making. Older lines there are the ones that crack, scale and need a sewer camera inspection.

Newer growth around Woodburn Premium Outlets, which opened in 1999, and Woodburn Estates, the largest 55 and over community in Oregon, sits on different pipe and different needs. If you live there, tell us the age of the home so we bring the right parts. Neighbors in Silverton and Salem reach the same 24/7 line.

Hubbard, Gervais, Brooks and the roads in between

Our dispatch also covers Hubbard, Gervais and Brooks, small communities near Woodburn. Hubbard and Gervais sit close by, and Brooks is a small community in the same stretch of the valley. Mount Angel is a short drive southeast on Oregon Route 214, which also carries traffic on toward Silverton.

If you call from one of these places, give a cross street or a landmark. It helps the plumber find you, especially after dark.

Code and licensing rules for plumbing in Woodburn

Plumbing in Woodburn follows the Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code, which is the state’s adoption of the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). As the permit table above notes, Marion County issues plumbing permits and does inspections inside the city, so the county is the office to call about a water heater, repipe or drain change.

You can check any plumber’s license through the Oregon Building Codes Division license search before you hire.
