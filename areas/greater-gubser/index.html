Reading the Greater Gubser boundary

The City of Keizer defines Greater Gubser with a long list of edges. On the north it follows River Road N, Country Glen Avenue NE and Lake Labish Creek. The east side is the city limits.

The south edge runs along Kinglet Way NE, Perry Street NE, Lucinda Avenue NE and Chemawa Road NE. On the west, Claggett Creek and River Road NE close the shape.

The city counts more than 2,500 households here and ties the area to the Gubser Elementary boundary. Those are the facts we use, and we leave the rest alone.

Creeks as boundaries, not as findings

Lake Labish Creek and Claggett Creek appear in the city description as edges of the neighborhood. We mention them to help you locate yourself. We are not saying they affect any home plumbing, and we have no verified data that they do.

If you have water in a crawl space or yard, the cause could be a plumbing leak or it could be ground water. A leak detection visit can help sort one from the other.
