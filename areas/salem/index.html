Salem’s water and your pipes

Salem draws its water from the North Santiam River, fed by Detroit Reservoir, and treats it at the Geren Island Water Treatment Facility, which has served the city since 1937. It uses seven slow sand filters, and the Frank Mauldin Ozone Treatment Facility, completed in 2022, added ozone treatment.

The city adds soda ash to adjust pH, which it says helps minimize risks to household plumbing. It also adds fluoride, and its 2024 service line inventory found no lead service lines to date. That is good news for the pipe leading to the meter, though pipes inside the house are your own.

Turner, just south of Salem, buys its water from Salem. If you live there and call us, the supply is the same as the city’s.

Parks, bridges and why dispatch asks for a landmark

Minto-Brown Island Park, at 1,200 acres Salem’s largest, and Riverfront Park make good reference points when a street name is unclear. Wallace Marine Park is a 114-acre park on the West Salem side. Giving dispatch a park or bridge as a landmark helps the plumber find you.

Interstate 5 runs through Salem, and Oregon Routes 22, 99E, 213, 221 and 51 also serve the city. A call from the Keizer border to the middle of the city takes minutes. We also serve Woodburn to the north and Silverton to the east.

Permits in Salem

The City of Salem says electrical, plumbing, mechanical and fire work are applied for online in its Permit Application Portal. If the applicant is not the contractor, the contractor doing the work must sign a contractor authorization form before the permit is issued.

We handle the portal steps when your job needs a permit, and you can verify any plumber’s license with the Oregon Building Codes Division license search.

Code and licensing rules for plumbing in Salem

Plumbing in Salem follows the Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code, which is the state’s adoption of the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). As the permit section above explains, the City of Salem uses an online Permit Application Portal, and the contractor doing the work signs an authorization form.

You can check any plumber’s license through the Oregon Building Codes Division license search before you hire.
