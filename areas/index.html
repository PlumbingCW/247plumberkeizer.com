Keizer first

Keizer itself is ZIP 97303, with 97307 as the PO box ZIP. The City of Keizer reports a population of 39,561 across 7.36 square miles, so most addresses are only minutes from our base on River Road North.

Giving dispatch a cross street or a landmark such as Chemawa Road, Keizer Rapids Park or Claggett Creek helps a plumber find you quickly.

Smaller communities nearby

We also take calls from Hubbard, Gervais, Brooks, Mount Angel and Hayesville. These small places do not have their own pages, but the same 24/7 line serves them. Tell dispatch the town and a cross street.

Drive times from our Keizer base are estimates and change with traffic, so dispatch gives you a window rather than a promise.
