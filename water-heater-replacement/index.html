When a failed water heater becomes an emergency

A tank that leaks from its shell, rusty hot water that will not clear or a unit with a hot relief valve is a failed heater. Our 24/7 line takes the call at any hour and arranges a replacement visit as soon as a crew is free, including nights and weekends.

Shut the cold supply valve at the top of the tank, turn off the power or gas, and open one hot faucet. This limits the damage while you wait.

  • ✓
    Close the cold valve. It stops the tank from refilling and slows a leak.
  • ✓
    Kill power or gas. Use the breaker or the gas control. Never leave a dry electric element energized.
  • ✓
    Clear the floor. Move boxes and electronics away from the base and sop up standing water.
  • ✓
    Photograph the unit. A photo of the label with the model and size helps dispatch plan the replacement.

How we choose the right replacement

The right unit matches your household, your fuel and your space. Size is set by how many people use hot water at the same time, not by the size of the house. Fuel is usually the same as the old unit unless you want to change it.

Heat pump water heaters use far less electricity than a standard electric tank. Energy Trust of Oregon lists water heater incentives on its website, so check its page and your electric utility before buying. We do not promise rebates, and you should confirm eligibility first.

Clearance matters for a heat pump unit, since it needs air space and a drain for condensate. We check the closet or garage before recommending one.

Repair or replace: the honest answer

If your heater is leaking from the shell, there is no repair that lasts, so replace it. If it simply lost heat and the tank is sound, water heater repair may save money. Ask for both numbers and decide with the facts in front of you.

Code points that shape a replacement

A replacement has to meet the Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code, which is the state’s adoption of the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). In practice that means a relief valve with a proper discharge pipe, correct venting for gas units, and thermal expansion control when the home’s water system is closed to the street.

The manufacturer’s installation instructions set clearances, venting and fuel connections, and they take priority over habit. Ask your plumber to name the permit and inspection steps for your address before the old tank comes out.
